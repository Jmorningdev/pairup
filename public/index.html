PairUp

Running a mentoring program? Paste who each mentee wants, in order, and how many mentees each mentor can take. PairUp makes a stable pairing — no mentee and mentor would both rather be with each other — and tells each person why they got who they got. Nothing leaves your browser.

One per line: Name: 1st choice, 2nd choice, … — or paste rows copied from a spreadsheet (name column, then choice columns; a header row is fine).

One per line: Name (2) = takes 2 mentees. Optional ranking: Name (2): top mentee, … Spreadsheet rows with a capacity column work too.

PairUp uses the deferred-acceptance (Gale–Shapley) method, mentee-proposing. Open source under the MIT License: source on GitHub.
